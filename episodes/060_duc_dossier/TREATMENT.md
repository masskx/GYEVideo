# Treatment: 060_duc_dossier

## 1. Three candidate structures
1. **Mugshot line-up**: five -duce words stand in a line-up with one impostor (*pride*); the root exposes it. Strong gag, but it teaches "spot the fake" rather than "solve the question".
2. **Declassified passage**: a short reading passage with five words redacted; each lifts as the root is explained. Closest to 阅读, but too much text for a 60 s vertical frame.
3. **Case investigation**: a cloze question with four look-alike options is the crime scene; the root is the fingerprint; each word is an exhibit; we come back and solve it.

**Chosen: 3**, because it opens on the exact exam pain (four -duce options), pays it off in the same frame (bookend), and leaves a second question for the comments.

## 2. Logline and arc
- Logline: Four cloze options look like quadruplets until one fingerprint, *duc = lead*, tells them apart.
- Setup: the question and its four suspects. Turn: they share a fingerprint, and the prefix says which way you lead. Ending: the case is stamped closed; a new file arrives with its answer still blacked out.

## 3. Benchmark
- Reference: police-procedural title cards and case-file graphics in pop-art print (Ben-Day dots, rubber stamps); Japanese variety-show telops for the keyword caption.
- Learn: one exhibit per card, the "file" framing, hard stamp hits as punctuation, captions that highlight one word.
- Don't take: any show's characters, logos, fonts, layouts or music.

## 4. Shot list

| 1 | cloze-case | full page | slow push-in | ~9 | scrutiny; the question is the hook |

| 10 | solve | back to the case | push-in, then hold for the stamp | ~8 | bookend and verdict |

## 5. Signature shot and native move
- Signature: the pull-back from the `duc` card to the full evidence board, strings snapping out to all five exhibits.
- Native move at the peak: the silence, then the 结案 stamp slams onto the solved question (shake, flash, bell tag).

## 6. Cue map
- 100 BPM in D (minor family), scene starts snapped to the half-beat (0.3 s).
- Sections: case = sneak, fingerprint = secret, root to board = groove, solve = strip, homework = light.
- Silences: 0.4 s before the fingerprint line; 0.7 s before "选 reduce", so the stamp lands alone.
