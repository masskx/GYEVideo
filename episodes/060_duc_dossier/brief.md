# Episode Brief: 060_duc_dossier

## Root
`duc / duct` = `lead / 带领，引导` (拉丁语 ducere)

## Students
高一到高三。认识 produce / reduce，但在完形里分不清形近的 -duce 选项，只能靠语感蒙。

## The case (hook question)
- Stem: `The city plans to ____ plastic waste by half within five years.`
- 中文: 这座城市计划五年内把塑料垃圾____一半。
- Options: A. produce B. reduce C. introduce D. conduct (answer: B)
- Source: 高考风格原创练习题

## Exhibits
| # | Word | Level | Parts (gloss) | Literal | Exam meaning | Collocation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | produce (+ product) | 课标 | pro 向前 + duc 带 + e | 向前带出来 | 生产；产生 | produce food |
| 02 | reduce | 课标 | re 往回 + duc 带 + e | 往回带 | 减少；降低 | reduce pollution |
| 03 | introduce | 课标 | intro 往里 + duc 带 + e | 把人带进来 | 介绍；引进 | introduce yourself |
| 04 | conduct | 课标 | con 一起 + duct 带 | 带着大家一起做 | 实施；开展 | conduct a survey |
| 05 | education | 课标 | e 向外 + duc 引 + ation | 把潜能引出来 | 教育 | receive a good education |

## Homework question
- `Our class will ____ a survey on sleep habits next week.` A. produce B. reduce C. introduce D. conduct
- Answer: D. conduct (pin when 061 goes up)
