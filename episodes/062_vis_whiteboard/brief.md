# Episode brief: 062_vis_whiteboard

## Template
vocab-whiteboard (白板词根图) — first episode of the template; previous vocab episode 061_port_gameshow.

## Students
高二–高三. *visit / visible* are easy; *evidence* and *provide* show up in reading and cloze and nobody links them to "see".

## Content
Root `vis / vid` = see / 看.

| Word | Level | Parts | Literal | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| visit | 课标 | vis 看 + it 去 | 去看 | 参观；拜访 | visit a museum |
| vision | 课标 | vis 看 + ion | 看的能力 | 视力；远见 | have good vision |
| visible | 课标 | vis 看 + ible 能…的 | 能被看见的 | 可见的 | clearly visible |
| evidence | 课标 | e 向外 + vid 看 + ence | 让人看得见的东西 | 证据 | find evidence |
| provide (twist) | 课标 | pro 向前 + vid 看 + e | 提前看到 → 预先准备 | 提供 | provide food and water |

## Comment question
- Thanks to the new ____, the police finally found the thief. A. vision B. evidence C. visit D. provide — answer B.
