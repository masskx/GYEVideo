# Treatment: 062_vis_whiteboard

## 1. Three candidate structures
1. **Word map with a twist**: root in the centre, four easy branches, one surprising word (provide) whose wrong guess is erased, pull back to the whole map.
2. **Twist first**: open on "provide 和看有什么关系？", then build the map to answer it.
3. **Two roots, one board**: vis vs spect side by side.

**Chosen:** 1 — the first whiteboard episode teaches the board grammar; the twist gives the second half its turn.

## 2. Logline and arc
- Logline: one Chinese word on a whiteboard — 看 — and five exam words grow out of it.
- Setup (看) → four branches build confidence → the twist breaks the pattern → the overview shows the whole map → homework on a magnet card.

## 3. Hook (first 3 seconds)
- First frame: an empty whiteboard corner; 看 is written in marker as the voice says it.
- First line: 今天在白板上，只讲一个字：看。

## 4. Shot list
| # | Scene kind | What we see | Motion | Seconds | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | title | 看, 高考词汇 白板 | handwriting | 3 | the one idea |

| 3–6 | branch ×4 | arrow, word, parts (blue), literal, meaning (red), example | camera travels slot to slot | 5–6 each | the pattern |
| 7 | twist | provide, 提供, wrong guess → eraser → parts | camera to bottom centre | 10 | the turn |

## 5. Signature shot
- The overview pull-back: the whole word map readable on a phone.

## 6. Cue map
- Tempo: 92 BPM, key F.
- Silence: 0.6 s before the twist answer (`pauseBefore`).
- `cueAt` tokens finish each write (V I D, 去看, 参观, …).
