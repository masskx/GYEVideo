# Episode brief: 061_port_gameshow

## Template
vocab-game-show (词根闯关) — the first episode of the template; the previous vocab episode was 060_duc_dossier.

## Students
高一–高三. They know *import / export* from Chinese trade news but mix up *support / report*, and lose points on cloze items that turn on the prefix.

## Content
Root `port` = carry / 拿，运 (Latin *portare*).

| Word | Level | Prefix (gloss) | Literal | Meaning | Collocation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| import | 课标 | im 向里 | 往里运 | 进口 | import goods 进口商品 |
| export | 课标 | ex 向外 | 往外运 | 出口 | export products 出口产品 |
| transport | 课标 | trans 跨过 | 运过去 | 运输 | public transport 公共交通 |
| support | 课标 | sup 在下面 | 从下面托着 | 支持 | support each other 互相支持 |
| report | 课标 | re 回来 | 把消息带回来 | 报告；报道 | report the news 报道新闻 |

## Comment question
- The country has no oil fields, so it has to ____ all its oil. A. import B. export C. support D. report — answer A.
- Source: 高考风格原创练习题
