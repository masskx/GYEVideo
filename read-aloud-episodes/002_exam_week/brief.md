## Target Format

- 960x720, 30fps.
- 25-35 seconds.
- The audio directly reads the mixed Chinese-English copy.
- Chinese parts are read in Chinese; embedded target words are read in English.
- Do not add a spoken opening hook, separate explanation, or teaching commentary.
- Top challenge progress bar.
- Middle high-density Chinese story board with embedded English words.
- English words use black/yellow brand highlight plus red underline/mark.
- Inline Chinese gloss appears progressively as each word is read.
- Bottom word card switches word by word from real voice timeline.
- Bottom music-player decoration stays visible.
