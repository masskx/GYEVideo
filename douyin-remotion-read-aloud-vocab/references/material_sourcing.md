# Material Sourcing

## Good Source Material

This format works best when the source has a concrete life scene and an emotional curve.

Good scenes:

- early class after staying up late,
- exam week collapse,
- fake library study,
- thesis or assignment deadline,
- Monday office fatigue,
- diet failure,
- social anxiety before a presentation,
- saving money but still impulse buying,
- trying to sleep but doomscrolling.

Weak scenes:

- abstract word lists,
- generic motivational quotes,
- grammar explanations,
- long textbook passages,
- topics with no emotional conflict.

## Where To Look

Use sources that provide scenes, not just vocabulary:

- your own daily observations and comments from viewers,
- Xiaohongshu / Douyin comment pain points,
- school or work memes,
- short English passages that already have mood and action,
- IELTS/TOEFL/CET reading snippets only after rewriting them into a Chinese life scene,
- existing word episodes from this repository when they can be converted into a story.

Do not copy a platform creator's exact script. Extract the structure, emotion, and word opportunity, then write a new original story.

## Material Selection Test

Before creating an episode, answer:

- Can one viewer say “this is me” in the first 2 seconds?
- Can 10-12 target words fit naturally inside one Chinese paragraph?
- Does each word carry a scene role, not just a dictionary meaning?
- Can the full mixed Chinese-English script be read in 25-35 seconds?
- Does the ending leave a small emotional turn or punchline?

Reject the material if two or more answers are “no”.

## Conversion Method

1. Pick one scene and one emotional path.
2. Write the story in Chinese first.
3. Replace 10-12 key Chinese words or phrases with English target words.
4. Keep the sentence readable even if the viewer does not know the English word.
5. For each English word, write the bottom-card explanation:
   - dictionary meaning,
   - scene phrase,
   - why it is used here.
6. Split the voice script by target word. Each `word-<id>` line reads the Chinese phrase that contains that word.
7. Do not add separate spoken explanation. The screen explains; the audio reads.

## Reusable Episode Ideas

- `exam_week_burnout`: cramming, panic, fragile routine, caffeine loop.
- `office_monday_shutdown`: commute, inbox, performance, exhaustion.
- `library_fake_study`: pretend focus, distraction, anxiety, deadline.
- `diet_failure_day`: craving, restraint, collapse, regret.
- `presentation_anxiety`: rehearse, freeze, vulnerable, recover.
- `saving_money_fail`: budget, temptation, justify, abandon plan.
