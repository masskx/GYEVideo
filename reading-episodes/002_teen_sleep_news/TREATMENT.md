# Treatment: 002_teen_sleep_news

## 1. Three candidate structures
1. **Expert → surprising number → mechanism → consequence → step**, then question and recap.
2. **Myth → fact**: "Staying up late to study helps" — then the facts break it.
3. **Number-first cold open**: "1 in 4." on black, then the story behind it.

**Chosen:** 1 — the clearest Gaokao-passage shape (主旨 question), and each chunk has one graphic.

## 2. Logline and arc
- Logline: three out of four high-school students don't sleep enough — and it is costing them the memory they study for.
- What experts say → how few do it → why it matters → the cost → one step.

## 3. Hook (first 3 seconds)
- First frame: SCIENCE kicker, *Why Teens Need More Sleep* slams word by word.
