# Treatment: 001_screen_promise

## 1. Three candidate structures
1. **Problem → fix story** in five pages, words recap, question with evidence.
2. **Diary of one day**: morning, class, evening, midnight, next morning.
3. **Two endings**: the pig chooses; viewers vote in the comments.

**Chosen:** 1 — the clearest arc for a first episode, and the question can point to one sentence.

## 2. Logline and arc
- Logline: Little Pig keeps promising "ten more minutes" — until a lost lesson makes him change.
- Habit → consequence → decision → change.

## 3. Hook (first 3 seconds)
- First frame: crayon bedroom at night, a glowing phone, the title handwritten; the pig lands.
- First line: Little Pig and the Midnight Screen.

## 4. Shot list
| # | Scene kind | What we see | Motion | Seconds | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | cover | bedroom-night, phone, moon | title write, pig lands | 4 | the world |
| 2–6 | page ×5 | bedroom → bedroom (clock 12:00) → classroom → bedroom → street morning | page turn, karaoke, word cards | 6–9 each | the story |
| 7 | words | six words with phonetics | list pops | 8 | recall |
| 8 | question | options, thinking timer, answer, evidence box | silence 2.2 s, reveal | 12 | exam skill |
| 9 | end | park day, sun, tree, pig cheering | The end, comment prompt | 5 | warmth |

## 5. Signature shot
- The page turn into the classroom: the night ends, the consequence begins.

## 6. Cue map
- Tempo: 84 BPM, key G.
- Silence: 2.2 s before the answer (`pauseBefore`), clock ticks.

## 7. Sound design
| Section | Music state | Main SFX |
|---|---|---|
| cover, pages | music box + pizzicato story bed | page turn, sparkle on word cards |
| words | bell on the downbeat | pops |
| question | woodblock ticks, music out | tick, ding on the answer |
| end | music-box finale | chime |

## 8. Captions and keywords
- Target words highlighted lemon yellow as they are read.

## 9. Versus the previous reading episode
First episode of the new reading line; the discontinued read-aloud videos used a black/yellow word-card look, so every point differs.
