# Episode Brief: 014_i_am_boring

## Mode

Chinglish Trap + Kinetic Douyin Visual

## Topic

`I am boring 不是我很无聊`

## Trap Diagnosis

- wrongExpression: `I am boring`
- intendedMeaning: `我很无聊 / 我觉得闷`
- wrongMeaning: `我这人很没意思 / 让别人觉得无聊`
- whyItFails: `-ing 结尾是"令人…的"，-ed 结尾才是"自己感到…的"。boring = 令人无聊，bored = 自己无聊。说 I am boring 等于自黑"我是个无趣的人"。`
- naturalExpression: `I am bored` / `I feel bored` / `I'm so bored`
- simpleRule: `-ing 描述事物令人… | -ed 描述自己感到…`
- usageScene: `朋友问你怎么不说话：I'm just a little bored.`

## Hook Audit
