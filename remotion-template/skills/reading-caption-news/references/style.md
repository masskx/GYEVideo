# Style: 双语新闻阅读 (Caption News)

Captions-first social news: the words are the picture. Dark grounds, heavy white captions, one yellow keyword, simple line graphics.

## Colour

- Grounds rotate through `NEWS.grounds` (navy, charcoal, deep teal, aubergine) with a soft vignette and fine grain.
- Captions white; Chinese lines `#C9D1E0`; the keyword on the **brand yellow `#FFD23F` block** with ink text.
- Graphics: white outlines, yellow for the highlighted part, red only for a falling trend's end point or a wrong option.

## Layout

- HUD top: series chip 双语新闻阅读 + No., topic label right (mono).
- Graphic band at y 240–760, caption from y 820, Chinese under the caption, the SOURCE tag under the Chinese (in flow, never overlapping).
- Captions at 84 px Inter Black, at most five lines; the Chinese at 42 px.

## Motion

- Caption phrases slam in (scale 1.18 → 1 in 0.12 s) as they are spoken; unrevealed phrases keep their space, so lines never jump.
- Graphics draw on (clock sweep, people filling with a rolling percentage, trend line drawing).
- SOURCE tags slide in from the left just after the number is spoken.
- Hard cuts between chunks; no dissolves.

## The pig

- Lower right on the headline, the question and the recap only; it never appears over a chunk's caption.

## Sound

- Score palette `caption-news`: tight synth pulse with a low sub and kick/hat → woodblock ticks for the question → pad for the end.
- A hit on each cut, ticks while numbers roll, a ding on the answer.
