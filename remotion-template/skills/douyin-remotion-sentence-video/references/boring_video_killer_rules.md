# Boring Video Killer Rules

These patterns make sentence videos boring.

## 1. Abstract Opening

Bad:

`你重点跑了。很多人吵架不输在内容，输在语气。`

Better:

`对方突然回你一句：你刚刚什么意思？`

## 2. Mechanical Repeat

Bad:

`Not what you said.`
`How you said it.`
`Not what you said.`
`How you said it.`

Better:

Show:

- what you said
- how they heard it
- the rescue line

## 3. No Character

Bad:

`这句话可以表达语气比内容重要。`

Better:

`朋友不是因为你提醒她生气，是因为你像在审她。`

## 4. No Crash

Bad:

- give the correct line immediately

Better:

- show the wrong or awkward line first
- then rescue it

## 5. Only Card Layout

Bad:

- the entire video is black background plus big text cards

Better:

- chat bubbles
- typing
- read receipt
- strikeout
- resend
- second scene reuse
