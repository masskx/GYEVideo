# Chinglish Trap Rules

The pleasure of this format is:

`原来我一直说错了。`

## Every Episode Needs One Trap

A trap may be:

1. Chinese direct translation
2. wrong word choice
3. Chinese habit transferred into English
4. too-simple expression upgrade
5. fake advanced expression

## Every Episode Needs Strong Contrast

At least one of:

- you thought it was English, but it is Chinese wearing English skin
- you thought it was polite, but it sounds stiff
- you thought it was advanced, but it sounds awkward
- Chinese four characters cannot become four English words

## Every Episode Needs A Reason To Stop

The viewer should not only think:

`I know this phrase.`

The viewer should think:

`Wait, I might have said this wrong too.`

That means the opening should connect the trap to:

- a common learner habit
- a common embarrassment
- a common Chinese direct-translation instinct
- a common fake-English moment

## Forbidden

- treating a classic Chinglish phrase as self-sufficient hook material
- only teaching a sentence pattern
- only giving the right answer
- saying `this is more native` without saying what is weird
- opening with grammar terminology
- textbook examples
- knowledge-card visuals
- Chinese-only ending summary
