# Anti-PPT Rules

This skill most often fails by generating a polished knowledge card instead of a real short video.

## Forbidden Frames

- big title + small subtitle + center card + bottom summary, all static
- all information shown at once
- three example sentences displayed together for a long time
- a cover that looks like a lesson handout
- background that is only a black gradient with no scene cue
- no camera change and no rhythm change
- only "correct", with no "want to watch"

## Required Short-Video Feeling

- the first second must interrupt attention
- text should hit onto the screen, not sit on the screen
- the sentence skeleton must have a slam reveal
- A/B slots must have swap motion
- examples must appear one by one, not as a list
- every example must carry an emotional tag or scene cue
- there must be a short drill repeat such as `Not that A.` / `Just that B.`

## Pass-Fail Test

If a screenshot looks like a finished poster, it is not a qualified video frame.
