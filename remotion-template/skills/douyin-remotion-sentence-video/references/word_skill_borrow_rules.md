# Borrow Rules From Word Skill

## Principle

The sentence skill should not invent a weaker visual grammar when the project already has a stronger one in the word skill.

Borrow the energy, not the topic structure.

## Borrow These Systems

- first-3-second interruption logic from the word-skill `HookScene`
- motion helpers such as `slamIn`, `punchZoom`, `shake`, `flashOpacity`, `highlightSweep`, `quickCut`
- active-state emphasis instead of flat static display
- flash-word bursts when multiple emotional labels or contrast cues help the hook
- grid / relation thinking when showing sentence part relationships
- short reaction beats when they strengthen contrast

## Translate Them Into Sentence Context

Examples:

- word-skill flash words -> sentence-skill emotional flash tags or trap labels
- root reveal slam -> natural sentence rescue slam
- word grid connect -> slot relationship or contrast network
- summary scene punch -> wrong-vs-right final lock

## Do Not Copy Blindly

- do not turn sentence videos into vocabulary lists
- do not force root-word pacing into emotional correction videos
- do not add reaction elements that compete with the sentence itself

## Final Test

If the sentence episode is placed next to a word-skill episode from the same project:

- would its motion feel equally intentional?
- would its opening feel equally interruptive?
- would its payoff feel equally dominant?

If not, rewrite the plan.
