# Chinglish Trap Opening Types

## First Principle

Do not mistake a phrase reveal for a hook.

`Good good study?`

by itself is not yet a Douyin hook.

It becomes a hook only when it is attached to one of:

- `你还在把中文硬翻成英文？`
- `你以为这句是英语，其实不是。`
- `很多人一开口就卡在这种中式英语。`
- `这句能当梗，别当正式英语。`

Do not default to a big title slide.

Use one of these openings:

## 1. Direct Slap

- `Good good study? 这不是英语。`
- `Open the light? 老外一听就知道你在直译。`
- `I am boring? 不是我很无聊，是我这个人很无聊。`

## 2. Chinese Pain Point

- `你还在把中文硬翻成英文？`
- `很多人会背单词，但一开口还是中式英语。`
- `这句你是不是也说过？`

## 3. Guess Trap

- `开灯你是不是想说 Open the light?`
- `错。`
- `说 Turn on the light.`

## 4. Wrong Meaning Twist

- `I am boring 不是我无聊。`
- `是：我是个无聊的人。`

## 5. Native Fix Slam

- `别说 Open the light.`
- `说 Turn on the light.`

## Rules

- the first line should preferably be Chinese pain point, social embarrassment, or reversal
- the first 3 seconds must contain the wrong expression or wrong guess
- the first 3 seconds must also contain the point of the trap, not only the phrase
- the first 5 seconds must contain contrast
- the correct expression must appear within 12 seconds
- do not start with `今天学习`
- do not split a wrong phrase into 2 to 3 beats unless the pain point is already on screen
- if the opening still looks like a cover image when paused, rewrite it
