# Douyin Hook Audit Rules

## Core Test

Ask three questions:

1. if the viewer sees only the first second, would they stop?
2. if the viewer watches muted, would they still understand the trap?
3. is the opening a pain point, or just a phrase reveal?

If the answer to question 3 is `just a phrase reveal`, fail it.

## Hook Must Include At Least One

- Chinese pain point
- social embarrassment
- reversal
- high-probability learner mistake

## Hook Should Not Be

- a cover title pasted into the video
- a quiz card with no emotional stake
- just the wrong English phrase on black background
- a split reveal that delays the actual point

## Good Signs

- the viewer immediately feels `this might be me`
- the wrong phrase and the reason-to-care appear together inside 3 seconds
- the opening can be understood from a screenshot

## Bad Signs

- the first 3 seconds can be summarized as `a phrase appears`
- the viewer must wait until second 4 or 5 to know why this matters
- the opening feels calmer than the middle of the video
