# Audio Rules

- The narration should sound like a short-video creator, not a classroom teacher.
- Split voice into short segments by beat, not by paragraph.
- Drill lines should be recorded as short, repeatable units.
- Sentence episodes should keep a strong rhythmic BGM bed instead of quiet explainer music.
- Default configured BGM target is `0.33`.
- Acceptable default setup window is `0.32` to `0.34`.
- Keep BGM ducked under narration instead of dropping the configured BGM bed to a weak level.
- If speech is dense, increase ducking depth during active voice while preserving the stronger BGM pulse between lines.
- The BGM should feel noticeable enough to support Douyin-style edit energy even when the voice is the main information layer.
- Use `DASHSCOPE_API_KEY` from the environment.
