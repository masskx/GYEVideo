# Entertainment First Rules

The viewer is not entering a classroom.
The viewer is scrolling.

So every episode must contain at least one:

- emotional identification
- roast
- sting contrast
- mini-drama
- follow-me challenge

## Lower Knowledge Density

Do not try to explain everything.

Avoid:

- long usage notes
- too many examples
- textbook scenes
- explicit grammar lecture tone
- “let's look at some examples”

Good target:

The viewer feels entertained first, then feels they learned by accident.
