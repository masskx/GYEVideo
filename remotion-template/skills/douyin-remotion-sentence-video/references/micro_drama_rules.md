# Micro-Drama Rules

Sentence video should feel like a tiny short drama, not a knowledge card.

## Every Episode Needs 4 Elements

1. scene
2. conflict
3. wrong expression
4. rescue expression

## Standard Structure

0-3s:

- enter the scene immediately
- no title page

3-8s:

- show the wrong English or bad direct translation instinct

8-15s:

- reveal the rescue English

15-28s:

- reuse the rescue line in one more small scene

28-40s:

- lock one natural English line plus one short Chinese explanation

## Core Rule

The viewer should remember:

`以后这个场景我可以这样说。`

Not:

`这个视频一直在喊一句英文。`
