# Natural English Rules

All English must be natural first, catchy second.

## Do Not Break English Just To Make It Short

Bad:

`Not what you said.`
`How you said it.`

Better:

`It's not what you said.`
`It's how you said it.`

Bad:

`Say solid.`

Better:

`Say: That's solid.`

Bad:

`Didn't mean to hurt you.`
`Just trying to honest.`

Better:

`I didn't mean to hurt you.`
`I was just trying to be honest.`

## Naturalness Checklist

Every English line should pass:

- sounds like something a real person would say
- grammar is complete or naturally reduced
- does not look like broken AI fragments
- matches the Chinese scene
- could be sent alone in a chat

## Display Rule

You may split one complete sentence across two lines.
Do not break the sentence itself.
