# Text Rhythm Rules

Short-video text must behave like drum hits.

## Main Text Limits

Per beat:

- Chinese: 4 to 10 characters
- English: 2 to 6 words
- longer English should split across 2 or 3 hits

## Correction Rhythm

Standard beat order:

1. Trap
2. Pause
3. Slap
4. Twist
5. Fix
6. Lock

## Voice Sync Rule

Each voiceScript segment must drive one visible beat.

Bad:

`这是中文逐字硬翻，能当梗，不能当正式英语。`

Better:

`这是中文硬翻。`
`能当梗。`
`别当正式英语。`
