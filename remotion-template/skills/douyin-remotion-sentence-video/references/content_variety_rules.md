# Content Variety Rules

Do not let every sentence video resemble `not A but B`.

The real enemy is not only repetition inside one clip.
It is account-level predictability across adjacent clips.

## Structure Mix

Use a batch mix such as:

1. contrast
2. instruction
3. emotional blank space
4. story turn
5. roast reaction
6. spoken filler

## Dedup Rules

Rewrite if:

- 2 adjacent episodes use the same opening shell
- 2 adjacent episodes use the same ending shell
- 2 adjacent episodes both depend on A/B contrast
- the memory point is still Chinese instead of English
- 3 adjacent episodes share the same emotional temperature
- 3 adjacent episodes share the same creator attitude
- cover titles use the same sentence grammar repeatedly
- the viewer can predict the reveal mechanic too early

## Variation Axes

Deliberately rotate:

- opening shell: pain-point shout, chat screenshot, roasty callout, awkward scene, fake confidence, comment reply
- creator attitude: sharp, amused, sympathetic, savage-light, older-sister, social-observer
- emotional temperature: sting, relief, flex, awkwardness, rescue, teasing
- reveal mechanic: strikeout rescue, message resend, roleplay flip, social correction, class divide, tone upgrade
- ending lock: wrong-vs-right board, one-line flex, reuse callback, challenge line, comment bait

## Sticky Batch Rule

In a batch of 5, at least:

- 1 should feel roasty
- 1 should feel socially useful
- 1 should feel emotionally relatable
- 1 should trigger comments or disagreement
- 1 should feel like a series opener or callback
