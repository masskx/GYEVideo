# Kinetic Visual Rules

This skill's videos must feel like Douyin, not courseware.

## Text Rhythm

Important text should be broken into beats.

Bad:

`Good good study, day day up.`
`真不是好好学习。`

Better:

`Good good study?`
`day day up?`
`真不是。`
`好好学习。`

## Wrong Expression Effects

Wrong expressions must use at least 2 of:

- red cross
- strike-through
- glitch
- shake
- crush
- `中式直译` stamp
- `NO` stamp
- warning frame
- distorted subtitle
- fake loading or error beep

## Right Expression Effects

Correct expressions should use:

- yellow slam
- punch zoom
- glow sweep
- word-by-word reveal
- `Say this` label
- send-message effect
- final lock

## Beat Density

0-3s:

- at least 4 visual beats

3-12s:

- explain why it is weird
- show the wrong meaning
- give the native fix

The correct expression must appear before 12 seconds.

## Forbidden

- long dual-column explanation freeze
- more than 18 Chinese characters on one screen
- whole long sentences sitting still
- a card not changing for 3 seconds
- fade in / fade out only
- only subtitle bar movement
- only particle background movement
