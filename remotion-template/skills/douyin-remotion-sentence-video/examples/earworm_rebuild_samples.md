# Earworm Rebuild Samples

## 003_didnt_mean_was_trying

- New direction: `dialogue`
- Opening type: `mini-drama`
- Earworm line:
  - `Didn't mean to hurt you.`
  - `Just trying to be honest.`
- Chinese memory:
  - `不是想伤你。`
  - `只是想说真话。`
- Why: the viewer should leave with the apology sound, not just `不是故意 A 只是想 B`.

## 004_instead_of_try

- New direction: `upgrade` + `drill`
- Opening type: `wrong-expression-roast`
- Earworm line:
  - `Don't say very good.`
  - `Say that's solid.`
- Chinese memory:
  - `别再 very good。`
  - `换成 that's solid。`
- Why: this should feel like a roast challenge, not a replacement lecture.

## 005_what_matters_not_but

- New direction: `chant`
- Opening type: `shock-contrast`
- Earworm line:
  - `Not what you said.`
  - `How you said it.`
- Chinese memory:
  - `不是你说了什么。`
  - `是你怎么说。`
- Why: the final memory point should be the spoken sting, not the abstract logic shell.
