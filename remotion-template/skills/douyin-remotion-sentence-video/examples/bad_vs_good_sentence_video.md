# Bad vs Good Sentence Video

## Bad

- every episode uses the same A/B shell
- the viewer remembers only the Chinese formula
- opening looks like a lesson cover
- no chorus
- no speed drill
- ending locks Chinese instead of English

## Good

- each episode has one English earworm line
- English repeats at least 3 times
- the opening type varies across the batch
- the rhythm type varies across the batch
- the ending leaves one English sound fragment in the ear
- the whole thing feels like entertainment with learning hidden inside
