# Chinglish Trap Examples

## 001_good_good_study

Trap:

`Good good study, day day up.`

Intended:

`好好学习，天天向上。`

Problem:

This is Chinese word-for-word hard translation.

Fix:

`Study hard and make progress every day.`

Short natural version:

`Keep learning. Keep improving.`

Hook:

`Good good study，不是好好学习。`

## 002_open_the_light

Trap:

`Open the light.`

Intended:

`开灯。`

Fix:

`Turn on the light.`

Hook:

`开灯不是 Open the light。`

## 003_i_am_boring

Trap:

`I am boring.`

Intended:

`我很无聊。`

Wrong meaning:

`我是个无聊的人。`

Fix:

`I'm bored.`

## 004_i_very_like_it

Trap:

`I very like it.`

Intended:

`我很喜欢它。`

Fix:

`I really like it.`

## 005_very_good

Trap:

`Very good.`

Intended:

`很好。`

Fix:

- `That's great.`
- `That's impressive.`
- `That's solid.`
