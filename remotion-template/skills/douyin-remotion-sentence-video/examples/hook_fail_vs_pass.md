# Hook Fail vs Pass

## Fail

Opening:

- `Good good study?`
- `day day up?`
- `这不是英语。`

Why it fails:

- first 2 to 3 seconds are only phrase display
- viewer sees the joke, but not the pain point
- feels like a title sequence, not a stop-scroll hook

## Pass

Opening:

- `你还在把中文硬翻成英文？`
- `Good good study, day day up?`
- `这不是英语，只是中文换皮。`

Why it passes:

- pain point comes first
- wrong phrase appears fast
- reversal lands inside 3 seconds
- even muted, the screenshot already communicates the trap

## Fail

Opening:

- `Open the light?`
- `Turn on the light.`

Why it fails:

- looks like answer card
- no embarrassment, no social tension, no trap feeling

## Pass

Opening:

- `你是不是也说过 Open the light?`
- `老外会懂，但一听就知道你在直译。`
- `说 Turn on the light.`

Why it passes:

- viewer is implicated
- contrast is immediate
- correction arrives fast without becoming class-like
