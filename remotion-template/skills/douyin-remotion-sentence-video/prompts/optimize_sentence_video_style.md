# Prompt: Optimize Sentence Video Style

Use the `douyin-remotion-sentence-video` skill.

Review the current Remotion episode and directly fix the most obvious Douyin-style problems.

Focus on these failure modes:

- card feeling is too strong
- it looks like PPT
- the first 3 seconds are weak
- the sentence skeleton appears too late
- A/B slots are not clearly swappable
- scene feeling is weak
- the cover has too much information
- the voice sounds too much like a teacher
- rhythm is too flat
- there is no drill-repeat section
- the usage scene looks like a two-column knowledge card
- the ending stops too abruptly
- the frame has large dead empty areas
- multiple episodes feel like obvious siblings from one template
- the bottom platform caption area overlaps with meaningful text
- the episode was rendered before a newer shell or safe-area improvement landed

When optimizing, you must:

- rewrite the first 3 seconds
- move the sentence skeleton earlier
- add or strengthen the scene metaphor
- reduce single-screen text density
- strengthen slot swap motion
- add drill rhythm
- redesign late scenes if they look like teaching cards
- give the ending a real closing shell
- activate negative space with atmosphere, line art, or reaction support instead of more text
- keep exactly one dominant focal answer in each late-stage card
- vary creator attitude, shell choice, and payoff rhythm when the batch starts feeling too samey
- rerender the episode if template-level shell or safe-area upgrades have not been reflected in the exports
- simplify the cover
- rerun the QA checklist

After changes, render key still frames and tell me what changed.
