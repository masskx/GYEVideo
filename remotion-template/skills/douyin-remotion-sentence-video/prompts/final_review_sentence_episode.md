# Prompt: Final Review Sentence Episode

Use the `douyin-remotion-sentence-video` skill.

Do a final delivery review of the current Remotion sentence episode.

Start by listing the Top 5 Douyin Problems, such as:

1. Does it look like PPT?
2. Is the opening not aggressive enough?
3. Does the sentence skeleton appear too late?
4. Do the A/B slots fail to feel like swap blocks?
5. Is there no real scene feeling?

Then fix the issues directly instead of only explaining them.

Check:

- The hook is compelling.
- The sentence pattern is immediately understandable.
- A/B slots are visually clear.
- The examples have emotion, scene, and reuse value.
- The episode starts from a real expression problem, not a grammar definition.
- There is a final memory formula.
- There is at least one drill-repeat section.
- The result feels like a short video, not a classroom grammar lesson.
- The usage scene has one dominant answer, not two competing lesson cards.
- The negative space feels designed, not unfinished.
- The ending has a clear final shell and does not cut off cold.
- The episode does not feel like a clone of the previous 3 episodes.
- The shell choice, line-art support, and closing beat feel intentional for this topic.
- No important text sits in the bottom platform caption overlap zone.
- If the template was improved after the last render, the episode has been rerendered on the latest template.
- Voice, subtitle, and scene timing are synced.
- `npx.cmd tsc --noEmit` passes.
- `npm.cmd run render` completes.
- MP4, cover, caption, and publish notes are exported.
- API keys are not written into source files.
