# Style: 白板词根图 (Whiteboard Explainer)

A clean classroom whiteboard, written live. It must feel handmade but never messy.

## Colour: three markers, fixed meanings

| Marker | Hex | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| black | `#23262C` | the words and the structure (circle, arrows) |
| blue | `#2A5CB3` | parts, glosses, literal meanings — the *process* |
| red | `#C8363A` | the exam meaning — the thing to remember; the rings that mark what matters (twist meaning, overview root), the 作业 label |
| highlighter | brand yellow `rgba(255,210,63,0.75)` | the root inside every word |

Board `#F7F6F2` with faint ghost marks of old writing and a soft sheen. No other colours on the board.

## Type

- Caveat Bold (Latin) and ZCOOL KuaiLe (Chinese): handwriting.
- A write reveals left to right with the marker tip travelling on the stroke; it lasts by length (`writeDuration`) and ends on its spoken token.

## Camera

- One continuous shot: no cuts. The camera travels between stops (`cameraFor`) and frames each branch inside the 980 × 1250 content area.
- The overview pull-back is the signature shot; it must show the whole map readable at phone size.

## The pig

- Screen-space, lower right (x ≈ 850, y ≈ 1290), beside the board content, pointing or thinking; it never stands on the board.

## Sound

- Marker squeaks on every write, an eraser rub, magnet clicks, the cap click at the overview.
- Score: style `lofi-study` (swung boom-bap, Rhodes) at relaxed / standard pace, `chill-pop` (plucks, marimba) at fast pace; title = intro, root = build, branches = verses, twist = break, overview = drop, homework = outro. The melody ducks under the voice so the marker sounds lead (`../../_shared/PACE_AND_MUSIC.md`).
